Mediterranean cooking, taken seriously.

Lavish opened in the heart of Stevenage Old Town with one idea: take the char, the marination, and the generosity of Mediterranean cooking, and give it the room, the ingredients, and the grill it deserves.

Everything of weight on the menu passes through our Josper — an enclosed charcoal oven built to hold in moisture while it sears. It's why the fillet arrives the way it does, and why the lavash comes off the grill blistered rather than merely warmed.
